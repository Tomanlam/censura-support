Censura Report Studio

Prepare course descriptions, subject comments and pastoral reports on iPhone and iPad.

Try the Sample Class

Choose Try Sample Class on the welcome screen. Open a fictional student to edit assessments, create an example comment, rehearse a grade save and export a PDF. Sample changes stay in memory and are discarded when you leave.

Work with your classes

Sign in through Han Academy ManageBac using an authorised teacher profile with an @hanacademy.edu.hk address. Choose Read my classes, select your reporting period and open a class. Its Members list shows the grades entered on this device. Select a student to write a subject or homeroom report.

Live AI generation requires an internet connection and a physical device supporting Apple App Attest. Review the inputs before sending them to the drafting service. To save Achievement, review the current and proposed term grades before confirming.

Get help

Email toman.lam@hanacademy.edu.hk with your app version, device, operating system and the steps that led to the issue. Omit student records, passwords and screenshots containing personal information. For school account permissions, contact your ManageBac administrator.
