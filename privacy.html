Privacy policy

Censura Report Studio for iPhone and iPad · Effective 25 November 2026

School access and local drafts

When you sign in, your credentials are entered in ManageBac’s school sign-in page. The app uses the authenticated WebKit session to read your teacher identity, classes and Members lists, including the names students use in English and Chinese. Censura keeps session cookies in WebKit. Signing out clears the school session. The fictional Sample Class works locally without signing in.

Grades, observations, curriculum selections and drafts entered in live classes are saved in files on your device, scoped to the teacher account. Draft files use iOS file protection and are excluded from device backup. Signing out preserves drafts for the same account. Deleting the app removes its local data. PDF exports you save elsewhere remain under your control.

AI drafting

When you choose to send a live generation request, the Censura report service on Vercel receives the inputs for that request and sends the prompt to Ollama Cloud for processing. Course requests contain subject and curriculum information. Student or homeroom requests can include a student’s name, pronouns, year level, ratings, selected skills, observations, and roles. The generated text returns to your device for review. You can edit reports locally and decline to send a request. Share only information the school authorises you to use.

The report service verifies the school tenant, teacher identifier, email, and a signed request from a genuine app installation. For Apple App Attest, the verification store retains the installation’s public key and an assertion counter to prevent replay. The app’s server code does not save report prompts or generated reports in that store. Hosting services may process technical information such as IP addresses and request timestamps to operate and secure the service.

Gradebook and exports

When you confirm a live Achievement save, the app uses your school session to write the selected grade for the selected student, class and term. The school controls those records and their retention. A PDF goes to the location you choose in the system file picker. The app contains no advertising or cross-app tracking.

Questions and requests

For privacy questions or requests about data held by Censura, contact toman.lam@hanacademy.edu.hk. Please omit student records and passwords from your message. Ask the school’s ManageBac administrator about access, correction or deletion of school records.
